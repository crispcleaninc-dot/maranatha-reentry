Workforce Partnership Agreement

Between Maranatha Reentry (a Michigan nonprofit corporation and IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) organization, doing this work as "Maranatha Works") and the partner company named below. Template for discussion. Each party should have it reviewed by its own attorney before signing.

This Agreement is made on  , 20 , between Maranatha Reentry, Detroit, Michigan ("Maranatha"), and   ("Partner"), located at  .

1. Purpose

Maranatha trains men returning from incarceration for hard-to-fill service work. Partner performs that kind of work. The parties want to give Maranatha participants ("Participants") paid work, supervision and a path to the licenses and credentials their trade requires.

2. How the partnership will work (check all that apply)

  1. ☐ Hiring. Partner will consider Participants referred by Maranatha for open positions and make its own hiring decisions.
  2. ☐ Subcontracting. Partner may send jobs to Maranatha crews under a separate written work order for each job, stating scope, price and schedule.
  3. ☐ Apprenticeship and licensing. Partner will provide on-the-job training toward this credential:  , including supervision required by the licensing agency.

3. Maranatha's responsibilities

  1. Refer only Participants who have finished Maranatha's safety basics and who chose this work.
  2. Provide each referred Participant with basic safety gear, unless Partner supplies it.
  3. Stay in contact with Participants for job-retention support (transportation, ID, housing, scheduling problems).
  4. Share Participant information only with the Participant's signed release, using Maranatha's release-of-information form.

4. Partner's responsibilities

  1. Licenses. Hold and keep current every license, permit and registration its work requires, and assign licensed work only to workers allowed to do it under those rules.
  2. Pay. Pay Participants it hires directly, on time, at no less than the wage it pays other workers in the same role, and never less than the law requires. Partner is the employer of Participants it hires.
  3. Safety. Provide training, protective equipment and supervision required by OSHA/MIOSHA for the work, including the Bloodborne Pathogens standard (29 CFR 1910.1030) for any work with blood or body fluids.
  4. Fair chance. Consider each Participant as an individual. Not reject a Participant only because of a record unless a law or license rule requires it for that specific job.
  5. Choice. Allow a Participant to decline trauma-scene or similar assignments without penalty.
  6. Notice. Tell Maranatha within two business days if a Participant is injured, stops coming to work, or leaves the job, so Maranatha can offer support.

5. Insurance and responsibility for harm

Each party keeps its own general liability insurance, and workers' compensation as Michigan law requires for its own employees. On subcontracted jobs, the party performing the work is responsible for its own work and workers. Each party will be responsible for claims caused by its own negligence or that of its employees.

6. Money

☐ No fees are exchanged for hiring referrals.   ☐ Partner pays Maranatha a training and placement fee of $  per Participant who completes   days of work.   Subcontracted jobs are paid per each signed work order within   days of invoice.

7. Privacy

Partner will keep Participant information confidential, use it only for employment, and not share it with anyone outside Partner without the Participant's written consent or a legal requirement. Partner will never ask Maranatha for a Participant's writing, journal or counseling information.

8. Conflicts of interest

Partner will tell Maranatha if any Maranatha board member, officer, founder or their family has an ownership or money interest in Partner. Any such partnership must be approved by Maranatha's board, without the interested person voting, and must be on terms no better for Partner than an unrelated company would get. Maranatha's charitable work cannot be used to benefit an insider's business.

9. Term and ending

This Agreement lasts one year from signing and renews each year unless either party ends it with 30 days' written notice. Either party may end it at once, in writing, for a safety violation, unpaid wages or misuse of Participant information. Ending this Agreement does not end any Participant's employment with Partner.

10. Other terms

This is the full agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Michigan law applies. Neither party is the agent or joint employer of the other's workers. Any disagreement will first go to a meeting between the two leaders before any other step.
