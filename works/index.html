How it works

Two ways we put men to work

Some jobs we do ourselves with Maranatha crews. Other jobs need trucks, licenses or equipment we don't have yet, so we train men and place them with licensed partner companies. Both roads lead to a paycheck and a credential.

Road 1

Maranatha crews

Maranatha runs the job, supervises the crew, and pays the men. Every dollar after costs goes back into training and the Reentry Home.

  • Cleanouts, junk hauling, trash-can and dumpster-pad cleaning
  • Pressure washing and bird-droppings cleanup
  • Adds licensed services as men earn the credentials
Road 2

Partner companies

Licensed companies that already do this work sign a partnership agreement with us. We send trained, ready men. They hire, supervise and help each man earn his license.

  • Grease trap pumping, septic and portable toilets
  • Pest control and wildlife removal
  • Biohazard and trauma-scene cleanup

For men coming home

From day one to running your own crew

You do not need to read well to do this. Every lesson can be read out loud to you in the Learning Center.

  1. Safety basicsLearn safety gear, lifting, chemicals and how to protect yourself from blood and germs. Short lessons you can listen to. Earn your OSHA 10-hour card.
  2. Join a crewWork paid jobs on a Maranatha crew or with a partner company, next to a crew lead who has your back.
  3. Earn a licensePick a trade you like. We help you study, pay the exam fee, and get to the test: pesticide technician, wildlife control, septage, biohazard and more.
  4. Lead a crewRun jobs, train the next man coming home, and earn more for it.
  5. Own itWith a license and a record of good work, you can start your own company. We help with the business plan, taxes and your first customers.

Where we are

Launching in three phases

Phase 1 · Start nowCleanouts, junk hauling, trash-can and dumpster-pad cleaning, pressure washing, bird-droppings cleanup. Needs insurance, a work trailer and safety gear, not a special state license.
Phase 2 · First licensesWildlife and dead animal removal, pest control technicians, kitchen hood and grease work. Men earn state credentials while working.
Phase 3 · Partners and big rigsGrease trap hauling, septic, portable toilets and biohazard cleanup, through licensed partner companies first.
